How to Request a Google Review the Right Way: Full UK 2026 Guide

24 September 2026

Asking customers for a Google review can feel daunting for any UK small or medium business. You know reviews are vital, but worry about coming across as pushy, awkward, or—worse—being ignored. Yet, done right, a steady stream of positive Google reviews can distinguish your business, boost your local reputation, and even improve your search rankings. Mastering how to request a Google review the right way is both an art and a science, and it’s more essential than ever in 2026.

Quick answer: The right way to request a Google review is to ask politely and promptly after a positive experience, providing a direct link and clear instructions. Personalise your message, thank the customer, and never incentivise reviews. This approach encourages genuine feedback and builds trust for UK businesses.

Key Takeaways

  • Personalised, well-timed requests are key to successfully gaining Google reviews in the UK.
  • Always use a direct Google review link and provide simple steps to leave feedback.
  • Respond to every review, positive or negative, to demonstrate professionalism and care.
  • Never offer incentives—this breaches Google and UK advertising rules.
  • Consistent, ethical requests grow your ratings and strengthen your UK online presence.

Why Google Reviews Are Essential for UK Businesses in 2026

The Role of Reviews in Consumer Choice

In 2026, online reviews are a make-or-break factor for consumers across the United Kingdom. According to Which?, more than 90% of UK buyers consult online reviews before choosing a product or service. For small and medium-sized businesses, Google reviews provide crucial social proof, reflecting real experiences and building trust. The more current and genuine reviews you have, the more likely customers are to choose your business over competitors with fewer or outdated reviews.

The Impact on Local SEO and Business Growth

Google reviews influence your business’s position on Google Maps and in local search results. The quality, quantity, and frequency of reviews all play a role in determining visibility—meaning that knowing how to request a Google review the right way can directly affect your bottom line. In sectors like property, finance, logistics, and legal services, reviews can be the deciding factor for prospects weighing up their options. For UK businesses, this can mean more footfall, more calls, and more conversions.

How to Request a Google Review the Right Way: Step-by-Step

1. Prepare Your Google Review Link and Instructions

To start, create a direct link to your Google review page using your Google Business Profile. Google’s official guidance explains how to do this. Copy this link and keep it handy for your requests. When you ask, include a brief explanation of how the review helps your business and clear steps for leaving feedback. Making it as easy as possible is one of the best practices for requesting reviews.

2. Personalise Your Request for Each Customer

Mass, generic requests rarely work in the United Kingdom. Instead, address each customer by name and reference the specific service or product you provided. For example, “It was a pleasure helping you with your tax return,” or “Thank you for using our courier service to deliver your parcels.” This shows you value individual feedback, not just your ratings.

3. Choose the Right Channel and Timing

The most successful requests come after a positive customer experience—right after delivery, project completion, or a resolved query. Email is the most common channel, but SMS, WhatsApp, or even a printed card can be effective if appropriate. Some businesses in the UK have seen strong results combining an in-person thank you with a follow-up email containing the Google review link.

What to Say When Asking for a Review: Phrasing and Templates

How to Politely Ask for a Review

British customers value politeness and subtlety. When thinking about how to request a Google review the right way, avoid language that sounds demanding or self-serving. Instead, use phrases like:

  • “We’d really appreciate your honest feedback if you have a moment.”
  • “If you’re happy with our service, could you leave us a Google review? It helps other people in the United Kingdom find reliable businesses.”
  • “Thank you for choosing us. A short review on Google would mean a lot to our team.”

It’s also important to explain why reviews matter. For example, “Your feedback not only helps us improve but also helps other customers make informed choices.” This frames the request as mutually beneficial and part of good customer service, aligned with the best practices for requesting reviews.

Review Request Email and SMS Templates

Here are two commonly used templates for UK businesses:

  • Email: "Dear [Name], thank you for [service/product]. Your feedback is invaluable and helps others in the UK find trusted local businesses. If you have a moment, please leave us a Google review here: [link]. Thank you for your support!"
  • SMS: "Hi [Name], thanks for choosing [Your Business]. We’d love your feedback—please leave a quick Google review if you’re happy with our service: [short link]"

Keep messages short, polite, and direct. If your customers aren't familiar with Google reviews, consider adding a quick step-by-step or offer to guide them through the process. Always thank them for their custom, regardless of whether they leave a review.

How to Follow Up for Reviews

If a customer hasn’t responded to your initial request, a gentle follow-up is appropriate. Wait around a week, then send a respectful reminder. For example, “Just checking in—if you found our service helpful, we’d still appreciate your review.” Never send more than two requests per customer, and always respect their wishes if they decline.

Tips and Strategies for Increasing Google Reviews in the UK

Making Review Requests a Habit

Consistency is vital. The most successful UK businesses don’t treat reviews as an afterthought but as part of their ongoing customer journey. Train your team on how to request a Google review the right way and set up reminders so every satisfied customer is asked. Add your Google review link to email signatures, invoices, receipts, and even business cards. The more integrated your approach, the higher your success rate.

Encouraging Customer Feedback Without Incentives

While it might be tempting to offer discounts or gifts for reviews, this is strictly against Google’s policies and UK advertising regulations. Instead, focus on genuine customer satisfaction. Let customers know their feedback is valued and will be used to improve your service. For example, “We’re always looking to improve and your honest review helps us do that.” Genuine gratitude goes much further than any incentive and is fully compliant with the law.

Handling Negative Reviews Effectively

Even if you request reviews the right way, negative feedback can happen. Respond promptly and courteously. Acknowledge their experience, apologise sincerely if warranted, and offer to resolve the issue offline if appropriate. This shows potential customers that your business is responsive and trustworthy. According to the UK government’s advice, addressing negative reviews openly can actually enhance your credibility.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Requesting Google Reviews

Violating Google’s Guidelines

One of the biggest errors UK businesses make is offering incentives for reviews, such as discounts or freebies. This not only puts your Google Business Profile at risk, but also breaches UK advertising standards. Always ask for honest, voluntary feedback—never request only positive reviews or try to influence the content.

Being Too Generic or Persistent

Customers can spot a mass message from a mile away. Personalisation is key for requesting Google reviews effectively. Avoid repetitive emails or messages, and never pester customers who haven’t responded. Instead, focus on building authentic relationships and delivering great service that naturally inspires positive reviews.

Ignoring Reviews After They’re Posted

Once customers take the time to leave a review, acknowledge it. Thank every reviewer—publicly for positive feedback, and with professionalism for negative reviews. This public engagement boosts your reputation and encourages others to share their experiences.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a direct Google review link for my UK business?

Log in to your Google Business Profile, search for your business, and select 'Ask for reviews.' This generates a unique link you can send to customers, taking them directly to your review page.

What’s the best way to follow up if a customer hasn’t left a review?

Send a gentle reminder about a week after your initial request. Keep your tone friendly and never send more than two requests. Thank them for their business regardless of their response.

Can I ask customers to leave only positive reviews?

No, you should always request honest feedback rather than just positive reviews. Asking for only positive comments violates Google’s guidelines and could harm your business’s credibility in the United Kingdom.

How should I respond to a negative Google review?

Reply promptly and professionally. Thank the customer, apologise if appropriate, and offer a solution or to discuss the issue further offline. This shows you value all feedback and builds trust with prospective clients.

What are the risks of offering incentives for Google reviews?

Offering incentives can result in your Google reviews being removed or your profile being suspended. It also breaches UK advertising regulations and can damage your business’s reputation. Always keep your requests ethical and transparent.
